# Limitations

- The geometry factor is constant and simplified.
- The assumed crack is not yet represented by a validated Abaqus model.
- The plane-strain-size screening fails for the default crack dimension.
- Material properties are deterministic and temperature-independent.
- Residual stress, weld effects and fabrication tolerances are omitted.
- Fatigue and corrosion-driven crack growth are omitted.
- Plastic collapse and interaction between fracture and collapse are omitted.
- Pressure and crack-depth colour bands are educational screening levels, not
  code-based acceptance criteria.
- The model is not compliant with BS 7910, API 579-1/ASME FFS-1 or DNV rules.
